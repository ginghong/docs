## Mission

DualClash aims to create a Web3-native public square where disagreement becomes participatory, legible, and economically expressive.

We want taking a position to mean more than clicking a poll, while keeping the experience simple enough for people who are not already crypto-native.

## What we aim to achieve

### Make conviction visible

Give each side a live, transparent market signal that shows how attention and committed support are distributed.

### Reward meaningful participation

Let participants contribute more than capital. Strong arguments should earn visibility and support from the people who have committed to that side.

### Turn positions into communities

Each stance token should function as more than an asset. It should help holders find peers, express affiliation, and develop recognizable roles within their side. Holding can signal membership; locking, arguing, or curating can signal deeper involvement.

The social layer should make identity and contribution legible without reducing status to wallet balance alone.

### Break out of passive echo chambers

Most social feeds optimize for content that confirms what users already believe. DualClash puts opposing positions in the same market, making the contrast unavoidable and giving each side a reason to answer the other.

### Make Web3 understandable

The user experience should begin with a familiar action—choose a side—then introduce tokens, locking, voting power, and fee sharing through clear consequences rather than financial jargon.

### Support open-ended topics

Not every important disagreement produces a final answer. DualClash should work for questions of belief, preference, policy, culture, and technology without forcing an artificial resolution.

## What DualClash is—and is not

| Category | Primary signal | Needs resolution? | Participant action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Social poll | Opinion count | No | Vote once |
| Meme launchpad | Attention and speculation | No | Trade a single token |
| Prediction market | Estimated outcome probability | Yes | Trade an outcome |
| **DualClash** | **Relative conviction and attention** | **No** | **Trade a stance, lock, argue, and vote** |

## Long-term direction

DualClash is intended to become a permissionless launchpad for public topics: anyone can frame a meaningful two-sided debate, communities can coordinate around their positions, and the strongest markets can grow into durable on-chain communities.
