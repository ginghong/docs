## Supporting capabilities

These are not the concept by themselves, but they determine whether the MVP feels complete:

- wallet connection and correct-network handling;
- transaction previews, pending states, confirmations, and failures;
- token, locked-balance, and voting-power accounting;
- argument ordering and eligibility feedback;
- empty, loading, disconnected, and low-balance states;
- responsive layouts for the primary target devices;
- a shareable link for a topic or argument.

## Likely later extensions

Keep these separate from the core experience until the MVP shows that the central loop works:

- permissionless topic creation;
- replies, quotations, and cross-side references;
- richer profiles, roles, badges, and reputation;
- moderation and community governance;
- token-gated side spaces or coordination tools;
- notifications and personalized topic discovery;
- market graduation or external-liquidity migration;
- mobile-native or embedded social experiences;
- composability with third-party communities and applications.
