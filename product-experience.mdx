The product should explain DualClash in one session: discover a topic, compare both sides, buy a stance token, stake it, and support the strongest arguments.

## Essential surfaces

| Surface | Primary purpose | Must communicate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Find an active topic | Clear question, two sides, relative activity, and leading arguments |
| Topic | Understand and join one market | Both tokens, market signals, arguments, and next actions in one place |
| Trade panel | Take or adjust a position | Output, execution price, price impact, fees, slippage, and wallet balance |
| Argument feed | Compare each side's reasoning | Side, author, rank, voting-power support, and reply context |
| Position view | Understand ownership and participation | Liquid balance, stake, voting power, allocations, rewards, and side identity |

## First-session journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand the disagreement">
    Show the question, both positions, and why the topic is active before asking for a wallet action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a stance">
    Explain what the token represents and preview the full trade before confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the position immediately">
    After the trade, update the balance, side identity, and market state, then offer staking as the next action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move from holder to participant">
    Separate liquid tokens, staked tokens, voting power, eligibility, rewards, and cooldown rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish or back reasoning">
    Make voting-power allocation visually distinct from an ordinary social “like.”
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Returning-user view

Returning participants should quickly see:

- price, supply, volume, holders, and activity for both sides;
- new and rising arguments;
- liquid balance, staked balance, and claimable rewards;
- allocated and available voting power; and
- replies, contribution history, roles, or recognition.

## Language rules

| Avoid | Prefer |
| --- | --- |
| “Winning side” | “Leading in economic support” |
| “Probability” | “Market attention” or “conviction” |
| “Like an argument” | “Allocate voting power” |
| “Stake to vote and earn” without detail | Separate stake, voting power, eligibility, cooldown, and rewards |

## What the product must validate

- Users understand the two-token structure without mistaking it for a prediction market.
- Buying feels like taking a position, not only making a wager.
- Staking creates a clear transition from holder to participant.
- Arguments give stance tokens more meaning than attention alone.
- Side identity and recognition create belonging without reducing status to wallet size.
- The market-and-content loop is useful enough to revisit and share.

<Note>
  The product needs a complete path through discovery, trade, participation, and return—not a large number of pages.
</Note>
